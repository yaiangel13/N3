20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90: se añade -ty

40 -> forty
50 -> fifty
60 -> sixty
70 -> seventy
80 -> eighty

Decenas: se añade un guión entre la decenas y el número
21 -> twenty-one
32 -> thirty-two
43 -> forty-three
54 -> fifty-four
65 -> sixty-five
76 -> seventy-six
87 -> eighty-seven
98 -> ninety-eight

Centenas
100 -> a/one hundred
200 -> two hundred
300 -> three hundred

Para unir centenas y decenas se utiliza and
255 -> two hundred and fifty-five
648 -> six hundred and forty-eight

Millares
1000 -> a/one thousand
2000 -> two thousand
10000 -> ten thousand

Millones
1000000 -> a/one million
2000000 -> two million
6000000 -> six million

Ej: 6279 -> six thousand two hundred and seventy-nine
