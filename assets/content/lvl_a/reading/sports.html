Sports

Eric and Pete are at a café and they're talking about sports and exercise. In this dialogue you will learn the names of many different sports and vocabulary related to games and sports

  • Eric Hi Peter
  • Peter Hi Eric
  • Eric Did you see the football match on t.v. last night?
  • Peter Yes! Sommerly was a much better team than Divan
  • Eric I agree. Their last goal was fantastic
  • Peter Are you still playing football?
  • Eric No, this year I'm playing basketball
  • Peter Oh really? That's great!
  • Eric And you?
  • Peter I prefer individual sports. I like running and cycling
  • Eric How often do you run?
  • Peter Twice a week. I usually run about 10 miles
  • Eric Good for you! Have you ever run in any races?
  • Peter Yes, last summer I ran in a 10 km race. It wasn't hard because I trained a lot before I did it
  • Eric Great! I prefer team sports. I like to play with other people and I am quite competitive. My favorite sports are football, basketball, volleyball, baseball and rugby
  • Peter I like watching individual sports on t.v. In the Olympics my favorite sports to watch are gymnastics, swimming, tennis and athletics
  • Eric I like tennis too. Let's play sometime!
  • Peter Okay, great!
